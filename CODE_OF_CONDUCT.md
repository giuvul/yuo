# Code of Conduct

Yuo is a project about learning, and people learn best where they feel safe to ask anything. We want taking
part in Yuo to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

This code follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new: every question is a fair question.
- Respect different opinions and experiences, and give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Act in the interest of the community, not only your own.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Insults, trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Giuseppe Vulduraro, through the contact details on
[giuseppevulduraro.com](https://giuseppevulduraro.com). Every report is reviewed promptly and handled in
confidence.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following
the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
